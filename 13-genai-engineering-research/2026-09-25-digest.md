# GenAI engineering research — 25 September 2026

First weekly shortlist. Sources below are preprints, and benchmark gains are author-reported within the stated evaluation settings. Treat each as a testable idea rather than a production guarantee.

| Area | Source and date | Finding and limitation | Why it matters | Try in a project |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Financial-services RAG | [Automated Regulatory Compliance Question Answering](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30009), 24 Sep | Domain-tuned retrieval raised Recall@10 from 0.256 to 0.774 on ObliQA. A high composite answer score still failed to demonstrate grounded obligations; transfer to Australian case law failed. | Bank-facing answers need verifiable support, not just fluency or a good aggregate score. | Build separate tests for passage recall, citation accuracy, and clause-level support of each obligation. |
| Tool integration | [Silent Failures in Agent-Tool Interaction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26836), 21 Sep | An audit of 15 scientific tools reported 91 manually validated failures, especially missing fields and inconsistent search/filter behavior. Results come from a specific tool environment. | A successful HTTP/tool call can conceal incomplete data. | Contract-test wrappers for required fields, pagination, filters, partial results, and explicit error signals. |
| Stateful agents | [TwinCheck](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26911), 22 Sep | In 159 paired BFCL V4 tasks, the proposed evidence-gated replacement raised author-reported success from 45.3% to 58.5% for one model. | Verification should have a concrete trace-level reason before modifying a tool action. | Replay tool traces and gate high-impact actions with state assertions and a specific failure hypothesis. |
| Coding agents | [An Empirical Study of Harness Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20804), 17 Sep | Across four models and 176 matched settings, planning, tool surface, and context management had model- and budget-dependent effects. | The repo-level coder agent needs its own harness evaluation. | Compare planning on/off, structured tools versus shell, and rule-based pruning before summarization on the same issue set; log solve rate, cost, and failures. |
| Agent security evaluation | [Attack Success Rate Is Not a Number](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25173), 21 Sep | A review of 259 papers found frequent omission of repeated runs and uncertainty estimates; cross-paper attack-success comparisons are poorly specified. | A single benchmark percentage may mislead security decisions. | Define attack success precisely; repeat stochastic runs and report variance, judge agreement, and intervention boundaries. |

## Suggested near-term exercise

For the existing bank-style RAG or tool-using assistant, select 20 representative tasks. Record retrieved evidence, tool inputs/outputs, answer citations, and final correctness separately. Add failure cases for incomplete API results and unsupported regulatory claims. This can become both a portfolio evaluation artifact and a senior-level interview story.
