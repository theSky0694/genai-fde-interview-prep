# Reported Interview Experiences

This section converts real/reported interview experiences into actionable preparation.

## Input workflow

Paste a LinkedIn post, screenshot, copied interview experience, or URL into ChatGPT. Each useful question will be researched, answered, categorized, and linked to the preparation roadmap.

## Every question entry should contain

- reported company / role when available
- source and date when available
- exact or carefully paraphrased question
- what the interviewer is testing
- 30-second answer
- detailed senior-level answer
- code / architecture example where useful
- likely follow-up questions
- common mistakes
- authoritative learning URLs
- mapping to the Master Roadmap
- current readiness and gap
- priority generated from evidence

## Evidence counters

For each normalized question/theme track:

- **Report count** — independent interview experiences containing the theme
- **Recency** — recent reports carry more preparation relevance
- **Company spread** — number of distinct companies reporting the theme
- **Role relevance** — GenAI Engineer / FDE / adjacent
- **Readiness** — current demonstrated preparation level

Do not interpret one LinkedIn post as proof of general interview frequency.

## Preparation Priority

Priority is evidence-informed, not a claim about the whole hiring market.

**P0 — Critical gap:** repeatedly reported/relevant + currently weak  
**P1 — High:** reported/relevant and important to target roles  
**P2 — Normal:** useful syllabus topic with limited evidence so far  
**P3 — De-emphasize:** low observed relevance after enough evidence accumulates

The roadmap may be reweighted when the reported-question evidence changes.
